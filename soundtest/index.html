Sound test

Every sound and music piece for the street game. Use headphones or the phone speaker. Tap the big button first (phones need one tap before sound works).

Main theme: sober ↔ drunk

One piece in three synced layers. Slide to get him drunk: piano alone, then bass and brushes, then the whole band. "Sober filter" muffles the world when sober (the game's state filter).

sobertipsydrunk

Whimsy builds the band

Each whimsy combo step adds a player: drums, bass, keys, horns, then the full parade. Break the chain and they drop out.

Moods

Tap a mood: the music crossfades to it over 2.5 s. Tap again to stop. Styles: street-jazz (the band), lone-guitar (lonely, grief), dream-piano (tender, whimsical), calm-piano (exploring).

The two of you (leitmotifs)

His line on guitar, hers on piano, written together. Both alive = duet. One dead = only the survivor's line.

Ambience

Loops. Turn several on at once to layer them.

Voices (mumble)

Speech-bubble gibberish, one blip per few letters. Type a line to hear it said.
